    How it works

    1. Upload the real bill, statement or plan.
    2. We confirm it with the biller and verify your ID.
    3. Your request goes into givers' swipe decks.
    4. Funds go to the biller. You get a receipt and can post a win.

    Prototype. All people, givers, bills, posts and payments on this page are fictional sample data and no money moves. A live version needs a licensed payments partner, identity checks, moderation of posts and, for any “back with a return” option, securities compliance.
